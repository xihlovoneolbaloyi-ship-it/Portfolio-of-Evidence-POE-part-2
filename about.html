About Sweet Crumbs

A small bakery built around good food, thoughtful service and joyful celebrations.

Our story

Sweet Crumbs began as a small home bakery in Johannesburg and grew through a love of baking for family, friends and local celebrations. Today, our goal remains simple: create delicious baked goods and make ordering them easy.

Our mission

To serve fresh, beautifully presented bakery treats while giving every customer a warm, reliable experience from enquiry to collection or delivery.

Our values

  • Quality and freshness
  • Friendly customer service
  • Creative presentation
  • Reliable preparation

Our promise

We treat every order as part of someone's story. That means clear communication, careful preparation and a finished product we're proud to share.
