## Working title
Sweet Crumbs Bakery – Local bakery brochure website

## Initial concept
A simple multi-page website for a fictional Johannesburg bakery. The site should help customers discover products, understand services and make contact without relying only on social media.

## Early goals
- Introduce the bakery brand.
- Show a basic product list.
- Provide a contact method.
- Keep the technical scope suitable for an introduction module (HTML, CSS, light JavaScript).

## Early page list (draft)
1. Home
2. About
3. Menu / Products
4. Contact

## Design direction (draft)
Warm colours, friendly tone, clear headings, mobile-friendly layout.

## Why this concept
Many small bakeries only use Instagram or WhatsApp. A small website gives a stable place for menu information and enquiries.

## Decision
This concept was developed further into the full Proposal B (`PROJECT_PROPOSAL.md`), which expands the site to seven pages, adds a gallery, services, a validated enquiry form, accessibility features and complete documentation required by the WEDE5020 rubric.
